1857 · The turning point

From Revolt to Republic

On 10 May 1857, soldiers at Meerut rose against the East India Company. Within months the old order was gone. This is the story of India after the revolt — the British Raj, the long freedom struggle, Partition, and the making of the world's largest democracy.

The Mahatma

Gandhi returns, and India changes

He came back from South Africa in January 1915 with a method — satyagraha — and a conviction that India could win freedom without hatred. Over three decades, the lawyer from Porbandar turned the Congress into a mass movement.
