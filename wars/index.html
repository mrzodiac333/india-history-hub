How to read this archive

One conflict, many perspectives

The main catalogue runs from 1686 to 1885, with a short prologue on the three founding battles of 1526, 1556 and 1565. It includes wars between Indian powers, wars against European companies and the Crown, and major armed uprisings.

Rulers are matched to the years of each conflict, not to a single date. Where a power was divided — the Maratha Chhatrapati and Peshwa, or Mysore's Wodeyar kings and effective rulers — both are shown. Britain's administrator is listed for context even when Britain was not a belligerent.
